02 The Field

37 acres of Kentucky ground

Point 6 is a recreational airsoft field in Foster, KY, built for first-timers, friend groups and regulars. Games are structured and refereed, and we care more about fair play than about winning. It's one of the first recreational airsoft fields in Kentucky, and players drive in from Northern Kentucky, Cincinnati, Ohio and Lexington.

03 Game Modes

A typical game day

Five rounds, each one a different mode. You get 30 minutes between rounds to reload, rehydrate and swap batteries.

  1. 1105–1130 OP-01

    Kill Confirmed Warm-up

    Land the hit, then confirm it. Played free-for-all or as even teams, depending on how many players show up.

  2. 1200–1245 OP-02

    Team Deathmatch

    Two teams, head to head. When you're hit, respawn and get back in. Lives are limited or unlimited depending on the round.

  3. 1315–1400 OP-03

    Infected

    One player, or a squad of up to 8, starts out infected. Everyone they tag joins them. Last survivor wins.

  4. 1430–1515 OP-04

    Conquest

    Points across the field are marked with colored flags. Your team has to capture them and hold them.

  5. 1545–1630 OP-05

    Capture the Flag

    Take the enemy flag and carry it back to your base. Keep yours where it is.

Know the ROE before you step off

Read these before your first game. Every player on the field is expected to know them.

R-01

Eye protection

Wear airsoft-rated eyewear at all times on the field. Kids have to wear safe eyewear too.

R-02

Hits

A hit anywhere on your body counts. Hits on your gun or gear don't, and neither do ricochets. Avoid head shots up close.

R-03

Medic

When you're hit, you bleed out after 30 seconds. To revive you, a medic keeps a hand on your shoulder for 10 seconds.

R-04

Respawn

Depends on the mode. You either go back to a set respawn point, walk 35 steps away from the gunfire, or stay down until a medic revives you.

R-05

Grenades & pyro

Pyro, spring grenades, claymores and smoke are allowed. Anything that could start a fire is not.

R-06

Lasers & lights

Red lasers and flashlights are allowed. Green lasers are banned. Never aim a laser at anyone's face.

R-07

Conduct

No vandalism and no violence. Don't shoot bystanders, houses or anyone who isn't playing. Be respectful to players, refs and neighbors.

Sign the online waiver before game day so check-in is faster. If you're staying overnight, there's a separate camping waiver.
